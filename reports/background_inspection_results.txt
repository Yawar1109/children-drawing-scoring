# Background Inspection Results

**Project:** Automated Scoring of Children's Hand-Drawn Shapes

**Inspection date:** 12 November 2026

**Inspection method:**
- Randomly selected 10 images from each of the 8 shape folders.
- Total images sampled: 80.
- Examined brightness and colour variation in the four image corners.
- Original dataset images were not modified.

## Conclusion

The initial corner-based inspection found noticeable pixel variation in all 80 sampled images. None met the script's near-white corner threshold.

These results are preliminary. They do not prove that the image backgrounds are non-white because the corners may contain shadows, pencil marks, or lighting variations. A more comprehensive pixel-brightness inspection is required.
